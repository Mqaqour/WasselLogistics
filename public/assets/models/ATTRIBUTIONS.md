freely-licensed (CC0 / CC-BY) glTF models. Files are used as-is (no rebranding, no
copyrighted airline/shipping-line liveries).

credited anyway as good practice. These are placeholders — swap in Wassel-branded
or licensed commercial assets whenever available, updating `MODEL_ASSETS` in
`components/story/modelAssets.ts` and this file accordingly.
